Home Healthcare Provider Permit
Summer 2026
City of Boston, Office of the Parking Clerk
Summer Fellow · Curb Transformation Initiative
Qualitative ResearchPolicy Analysis

Evaluating how parking access shapes the experience of giving and receiving home health care.

I co-led the evaluation, digitization, and reform of a city permit that lets home health workers park near the residents they care for. I built the persona discovery, data collection, and analysis infrastructure for interviews with five stakeholder groups, from care agencies to family caregivers, and reconciled the partner's permit records against the City's own. The current-state report and recommendations are now informing the next round of curb access policy.

UNHCR Aid Targeting for Displacement in Afghanistan
Summer 2023
University of Chicago, Harris School of Public Policy
Data and Policy Summer Scholar · Merit Scholar
Geospatial AnalysisHumanitarian Aid

Turning mobility and survey data into district-level aid recommendations for a crisis still unfolding.

For post-2021 displacement in Afghanistan, I combined cellphone mobility data, geospatial data, and UNHCR household surveys to test how well the surveys represent displaced populations. Nighttime light turned out to be a strong predictor of displacement, so I proposed a privacy-preserving satellite approach to target aid proactively. The project earned a high score on the International Policy Capstone and the Community Engagement Award.
